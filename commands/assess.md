## 📊 Assessment Format

### Question Types
- **Multiple Choice** - Select the best answer
- **Scenario-Based** - Solve real-world problems
- **True/False** - Conceptual understanding
- **Ranking** - Prioritize solutions
- **Code Analysis** - Identify issues/improvements

### Scoring
- **Beginner (0-30%)** - Just starting, fundamentals needed
- **Intermediate (30-60%)** - Some experience, skill building
- **Proficient (60-80%)** - Strong understanding, expert development
- **Expert (80-100%)** - Deep knowledge, thought leadership

## 📋 Assessment Checklist

Before taking an assessment:
- [ ] Choose a category of interest

- [ ] Be honest about your knowledge
- [ ] Don't worry about getting everything right

---

## 💡 Assessment Tips

### Before
- **Relax** - This is for your learning, not judgment
- **Be honest** - Accurate assessment = better recommendations
- **Skip if unsure** - Guess, but mark for later review

### During
- **Read carefully** - Understand each question
- **Take your time** - Not timed assessment
- **Think deeply** - Consider edge cases
- **Ask clarifications** - Use context of questions

### After
- **Review results** - Understand your gaps
- **Accept feedback** - See areas for growth
- **Start learning** - Use recommendations
- **Retake later** - Measure improvement
