# User Discovery & Research Guide

Begin your product discovery process with structured user research to understand your customers deeply.

## 🔍 Discovery Framework

### Phase 1: Research Planning (Week 1)
**Goal:** Prepare your research methodology

What you'll do:
- Define research objectives and hypotheses
- Select target audience segments
- Choose research methods (interviews, surveys, observations)
- Recruit 15-25 research participants
- Create interview guide or survey

**Output:** Research plan document

### Phase 2: Conduct Research (Weeks 2-4)
**Goal:** Gather customer insights

Interview Structure (30-60 minutes each):
1. **Warm-up** (5 min) - Build rapport
2. **Context** (10 min) - Current situation, background
3. **Deep Dive** (15 min) - Problems, frustrations, needs
4. **Solution Exploration** (15 min) - How they solve today
5. **Vision** (10 min) - Ideal future state
6. **Wrap-up** (5 min) - Closing questions

**Interview Best Practices:**
- Listen more than you talk (70/30 rule)
- Ask "why" 5 times to get to root causes
- Avoid leading questions
- Let silence breathe - don't rush
- Take notes but stay engaged
- Record (with permission) for later reference

**Output:** Interview notes, recordings, transcripts

### Phase 3: Analysis & Synthesis (Week 4-5)
**Goal:** Extract insights from raw data

Analysis Methods:
- **Affinity Mapping** - Group similar insights
- **Theme Identification** - Find patterns
- **Quote Collection** - Best representative quotes
- **Need Prioritization** - Score by importance
- **Opportunity Identification** - Unmet needs

**Key Questions to Answer:**
- What are the top 5 customer problems?
- What motivates customer behavior?
- What jobs are they trying to accomplish?
- What's their current workflow?
- Where are the friction points?
- What would 10x their efficiency?

**Output:** Synthesis document with key themes

### Phase 4: Persona Development (Week 5-6)
**Goal:** Create detailed, research-backed personas

Create 3-5 personas including:
- **Demographics** - Age, role, company size, industry
- **Goals** - What they want to accomplish
- **Challenges** - Pain points and obstacles
- **Current Solutions** - How they solve today
- **Motivations** - What drives them
- **Concerns** - What worries them
- **Quote** - Representative quote from research

**Persona Document Should Include:**
- 1-2 page description (narrative)
- Photo (representative image)
- Real quotes (3-5)
- Day-in-the-life scenario
- Key behaviors
- Goals and frustrations

**Output:** 3-5 detailed persona documents

### Phase 5: Journey Mapping (Week 6-7)
**Goal:** Map the current customer experience

Create Current State Journey Map:
- **Stages** - Key phases in customer journey
- **Actions** - What customer does
- **Emotions** - How they feel at each stage
- **Pain Points** - Frustrations and gaps
- **Touchpoints** - Where they interact
- **Opportunities** - Where you can improve

**Map Sections:**
1. **Pre-Purchase** - Awareness, consideration
2. **Purchase** - Decision, buying process
3. **Onboarding** - Getting started, learning
4. **Usage** - Regular product usage
5. **Support** - Help and assistance
6. **Renewal/Expansion** - Continued value

**Output:** Visual journey map + opportunities list

## 📊 Research Methods Comparison

| Method | Depth | Scale | Time | Cost |
|--------|-------|-------|------|------|
| **Interviews** | Very High | Small | Medium | Low |
| **Focus Groups** | High | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| **Surveys** | Medium | Large | Short | Low |
| **Observations** | High | Small | Medium | Medium |
| **Analytics** | Medium | Large | Ongoing | Low |
| **Contextual Inquiry** | Very High | Small | Long | High |

## 📝 Sample Interview Guide

### Opening (5 min)
"Thank you for taking time today. I'd like to understand your current [situation/role/challenges]. This is a conversation, not a test. There are no wrong answers."

### Background (10 min)
- Tell me about your role and responsibilities
- What does a typical day look like?
- Who do you work with?

### Current Situation (10 min)
- How do you currently [solve X / approach Y]?
- Walk me through your typical workflow
- What tools do you use?

### Pain Points (15 min)
- What's frustrating about [current solution]?
- When was the last time you struggled with [problem]?
- How often does [issue] occur?
- What's the impact when [problem] happens?

### Desired Future (10 min)
- What would make your life easier?
- Describe your ideal [solution/workflow]
- What would you want to change?

### Closing (5 min)
- Is there anything else important I should know?
- Can I reach out if I have follow-up questions?

## 📈 Key Metrics from Research

Track these to measure research quality:

- **Interviews Conducted:** 20+ (target)
- **Personas Created:** 3-5 detailed
- **Pain Points Identified:** 10-15 core
- **Unmet Needs:** 5-10 significant
- **Journey Touchpoints:** 20-30 mapped
- **Quote Highlights:** 50+ representative
- **Confidence Level:** 80%+ alignment
