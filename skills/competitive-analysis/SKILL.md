# Competitive Analysis Skill

## Overview
Analyze competitors and market dynamics to inform product strategy.

## Topics Covered

### Market Analysis
- Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- Market segmentation
- Industry trends
- Market dynamics
- Growth drivers

### Competitor Research
- Competitor identification
- Feature comparison
- Positioning analysis
- Pricing intelligence
- Go-to-market analysis

### Strategic Frameworks
- Porter's Five Forces
- SWOT analysis
- BCG matrix
- Blue ocean strategy
- Jobs-to-be-done

### Battlecards
- Win/loss analysis
- Competitive positioning
- Objection handling
- Sales enablement
- Differentiation messaging

## Prerequisites
- Market understanding
- Research skills

## Learning Outcomes
- Conduct competitive research
- Build competitive matrices
- Create battlecards
- Identify opportunities
