# User Research Skill

## Overview
Conduct effective user research to understand customer needs, behaviors, and pain points.

## Topics Covered

### Research Methods
- User interviews
- Surveys and questionnaires
- Usability testing
- A/B testing
- Analytics analysis

### Qualitative Research
- Interview techniques
- Observation methods
- Diary studies
- Focus groups
- Contextual inquiry

### Quantitative Research
- Survey design
- Sample sizing
- Statistical analysis
- Cohort analysis
- Funnel analysis

### Synthesis
- Affinity mapping
- Persona creation
- Journey mapping
- Insight extraction
- Opportunity identification

## Prerequisites
- Product fundamentals
- Communication skills

## Learning Outcomes
- Plan research studies
- Conduct user interviews
- Analyze research data
- Present actionable insights
