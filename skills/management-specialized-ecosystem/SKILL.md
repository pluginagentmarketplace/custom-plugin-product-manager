## Real-World Scenarios

**Launching a New Product Feature**
```
Phase 1: Discovery (2 weeks)
- Conduct user interviews
- Define requirements
- Create user stories
- Design mockups

Phase 2: Development (4 weeks)
- Sprint planning
- Development and QA
- Regular demos
- Stakeholder feedback

Phase 3: Validation (2 weeks)
- Beta testing with users
- Feedback collection
- Iteration on feedback

Phase 4: Launch (1 week)
- Final testing
- Launch coordination
- Monitor metrics
- Customer support

Phase 5: Optimization (ongoing)
- Analyze usage metrics
- Gather feedback
- Plan improvements
- Iterate
```

**Building & Growing a Team**
```
Month 1: Foundation
- Establish team processes
- Clear communication
- Define roles and responsibilities
- Quick wins

Month 2-3: Growth
- Hire 1-2 team members
- Onboarding and mentoring
- Team building activities
- Establish culture

Month 4-6: Scaling
- Process optimization
- Cross-team coordination
- Career development
- Performance reviews

Month 6+: Leadership
- Develop team leads
- Delegate leadership
- Focus on strategy
- Mentor others
```
